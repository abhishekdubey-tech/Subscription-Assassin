# Subscription Assassin — 2:30 Judge Demo Script

**Pacing:** Conversational, about 120–125 words per minute. Bracketed text is an on-screen cue, not spoken narration.

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

**[Show the landing page hero.]**

“One subscription rarely feels expensive. But forgotten trials, quiet price hikes, and charges across different services can add up. Subscription Assassin helps people find those recurring leaks before another renewal.”

## 0:20–0:42 — The product

**[Scroll through the animated spending story, then select “Start hunting” or “Enter the demo.”]**

“This is an evidence-first dashboard. The sample records are simulated, so you can explore immediately without linking a bank account. We turn a scattered list of charges into spending, renewal dates, and clear reasons to review.”

## 0:42–1:05 — Spending overview

**[Show the dashboard metrics and category chart.]**

“The dashboard calculates from saved records: thirteen active subscriptions, 208 dollars a month, or 2,496 annualized. Four are marked rarely or never used, representing 707 dollars and 64 cents in potential annual savings—not confirmed savings.”

## 1:05–1:30 — Investigate a price increase

**[Open Subscriptions, select Headspace, and show its price history and review score.]**

“Let’s inspect Headspace. Stored history shows 9.99 to 12.99 dollars per month, a 30 percent increase. Its review score also reflects the usage note. Each score shows its factors; it’s a prompt to review, not a command to cancel.”

## 1:30–1:52 — Insights that stay honest

**[Open AI Insights; point out the provider label and evidence.]**

“AI Insights explains patterns using saved data. When Gemini is available, it can help explain those patterns. If not, the app labels its rule-based fallback. Chat is read-only, so it cannot cancel or delete subscriptions.”

## 1:52–2:15 — Take action carefully

**[Open Cancellation Center and show a low-use subscription’s available method.]**

“In Cancellation Center, a low-use subscription becomes a candidate to review. Where there’s no supported cancellation API, we say manual cancellation is required. A status becomes confirmed only after the user says the merchant accepted cancellation.”

## 2:15–2:30 — Close

**[Show Settings briefly, then return to the dashboard or landing page.]**

“PayPal is Sandbox and merchant-scoped; it cannot reveal every personal-account subscription. Subscription Assassin helps people see, verify, and act on recurring costs—with control remaining in their hands.”
